Privacy Policy: Nigel Carreiro Publisher

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Nigel Carreiro Publisher is a private tool used only by its operator, Nigel Carreiro, with TikTok accounts he owns.

What data the tool handles

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens that TikTok issues when the account owner authorizes the tool.
  • Basic account information TikTok returns for posting (for example the account's display name and the privacy options available for a post).
  • The videos and captions the operator chooses to upload.

How it is used and stored

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are used only to upload videos to the account that issued them. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

Deletion

Revoking the tool in the TikTok app (Settings, Security, Manage app permissions) invalidates its tokens; the operator deletes the stored tokens on request or when an account is disconnected.
