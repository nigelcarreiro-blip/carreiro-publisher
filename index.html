Nigel Carreiro Publisher

A private publishing tool that Nigel Carreiro uses to upload his own short-form videos to his own TikTok accounts: Bet The Math, Strange Dispatches and The Failure Report Files.

The tool signs in with TikTok (Login Kit), and then uploads a finished video with its caption to the account that authorized it (Content Posting API). It is not offered to the public and is only used with accounts Nigel owns.
